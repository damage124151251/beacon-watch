# BEACON social kit

## Bio

A small crew. Always on watch. BEACON agents observe official service status, compare changes and keep a source-linked record. Every signal has a trail.

## Post 1 - The watch is on

A signal should come with a source.

BEACON runs three observation agents: Wick collects official service-status feeds. Echo compares the new reading with the last one. Log records what changed, when it was observed and where it came from.

Five-minute checks. Public observations. A trail you can inspect.

## Post 2 - Unknown is a state

A missing signal is not proof of an outage.

BEACON separates what a provider reports from whether its feed could be collected. Failed checks stay visible. Stale readings are marked. Previous observations remain attached to their source.

No invented all-clear. No manufactured downtime.

## Product facts

Provider-reported status, not independent end-to-end availability testing. Feed fetch time is not service latency. Deterministic agents, not LLMs or automatic repair. Scheduled work can be delayed by provider failures or infrastructure limits. State changes and samples are bounded by retention. The first observation is a baseline; it is not proof of recovery. No uptime guarantee, token value, trading returns or partnerships are implied. Videos illustrate the workflow and do not represent live incidents. No funds move through the status observers or the token watcher.
